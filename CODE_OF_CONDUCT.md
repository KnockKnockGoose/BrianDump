# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. The short form:

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests or discussions, should be able to do so without harassment, whoever they are and however they work.

## Standards

Do: be kind and patient, assume good faith, accept feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the people using the tool, and say sorry when you get it wrong.

Don't: use sexualized language or imagery, troll, insult, make personal or political attacks, harass anyone publicly or privately, or publish someone's private information without their permission.

## Scope

This applies in every project space and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Open an issue, or if the matter is private, open an issue that says only "code of conduct, please contact me" and the maintainer will reach out. Reports are handled promptly and as privately as the situation allows. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks these rules. The ladder is the Covenant's: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
